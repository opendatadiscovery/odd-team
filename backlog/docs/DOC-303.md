## Description

There are **two unrelated things** in ODD that both go by "ingestion filter":

1. **Collector-side regex content filters** — what `integrations/ingestion-filters.md` documents: regex `allow`/`deny` rules a collector applies to decide which entities it emits.
2. **The platform's `IngestionDataEntitiesFilter`** — a **token-auth WebFilter** on the platform side (the `auth.ingestion.filter.enabled` mechanism that gates `/ingestion/**`). odd-platform even owns a Concept named "Ingestion Filter" for this.

They share the phrase and **nothing else**. A reader who has seen `auth.ingestion.filter.enabled` (or the platform Concept) and lands on this page can easily conflate the collector content-filtering feature with the platform's ingestion-auth filter — concluding, for example, that configuring collector regex filters affects ingestion authorization, or vice versa. The page needs a short disambiguation note pointing the reader to the platform-side auth filter as a distinct concept (and the auth posture documented in DOC-301 / DOC-302).

Medium — a naming-collision clarity gap (Gate 2 — synonyms/homonyms), not an incorrect claim, but one that misleads through ambiguity.

**Failure shape**: homonym / naming collision — two distinct concepts share a phrase and the page does not disambiguate.
