    1. **Doc-side PRIMARY — `features/data-glossary/business-glossary.md`**: expand the Dictionary-tab section from three action verbs to a structured UX reference. Recommended structure:
       ```markdown
       ### The Dictionary tab — Catalog browse and search

       The Dictionary tab (`/termsearch`) is the catalog-wide entry point for browsing every term in the platform. The page composes five affordances:

       **(a) Term list — read-collaborative** — The list shows every term across every namespace, owned by any team. The list IS NOT filtered by your owner association or your team's namespace. This is intentional: the Business Glossary is a catalog-wide vocabulary, not a per-team artefact. If your operator policy requires per-team term visibility, treat term names + namespaces as the visibility boundary (a `finance/Customer` term is visually distinct from `marketing/Customer`); the platform does not enforce per-team scoping at this layer.

       **(b) Free-text search input** — The search bar at the top of the page filters terms by name + definition (full-text search). The search is server-side; results refresh on Enter or on the search-button click. The search runs against the entire catalog, not just your team's namespace.

       **(c) Filter sidebar — namespace / owner / tag axes** — The left sidebar offers multi-select filters across three axes: namespaces (e.g., select only `finance` + `marketing`), owners (e.g., select only terms owned by `data-platform-team`), and tags. Filters are combined with AND between axes and OR within an axis. Filters update the server-side search session.

       **(d) Infinite-scroll pagination at 30 rows per page** — The list loads 30 terms initially and fetches additional pages as you scroll. Large glossaries (hundreds of terms) are fully browsable but expect a perceptible pause on each scroll fetch.

       **(e) URL-backed share-ability — `/termsearch/{session_id}`** — The URL path captures a server-side search session that includes your search query and filter state. Share the URL with a teammate to restore the same view. Sessions are evicted after 30 days (see `housekeeping.ttl.search_facets_days`); a URL older than 30 days renders a broken page — bookmark URLs are not durable references.
       ```
