- **ADR-CANDIDATE-026** (NEW 2026-05-10B): Metric-storage backend selection is a strict bean-level boot-time decision via mirrored `@ConditionalOnProperty` annotations on per-MetricType extractor pairs — `extractors/external/*` (`havingValue="PROMETHEUS"`) and `extractors/internal/*` (`havingValue="INTERNAL_POSTGRES", matchIfMissing=true`); per-MetricType extractor classes (Counter/Gauge/Histogram/Summary) replace a single switch via `canExtract(MetricType)` dispatch, with `INTERNAL_POSTGRES` as the **default-on** mode via `matchIfMissing=true`
  - **Category**: promote
  - **Support**: surfaced by 1 sidecar (`CounterTimeSeriesExtractor.metrics.storage@L20`) — the convention is observed at 8 mirrored bean pairs (4 MetricTypes × 2 backends) per the sidecar's enumeration; the `matchIfMissing=true` on the INTERNAL_POSTGRES side is the load-bearing default-on decision

  - **Decision statement**: The platform selects between two metric-storage backends — `INTERNAL_POSTGRES` (writes to ODD's PostgreSQL `metric_series` / `metric_point` tables via JOOQ) and `PROMETHEUS` (remote-writes to an operator-configured Prometheus instance via `/api/v1/write`) — at boot time via mirrored `@ConditionalOnProperty` on per-MetricType extractor pairs. The decision has two structural sub-parts: (1) **default-on selection** — `matchIfMissing=true` lives on the INTERNAL_POSTGRES side, encoding "operator who never sets `metrics.storage` runs the internal backend"; only an explicit `metrics.storage=PROMETHEUS` flips to remote-write; (2) **per-MetricType extractor dispatch** — Counter/Gauge/Histogram/Summary each have their own pair of beans, dispatched at write-time via `extractors.stream().filter(e -> e.canExtract(type)).findFirst()` (`ExternalIngestionMetricsServiceImpl.getExtractor`). The `canExtract(MetricType)` interface method is the extensibility seam: adding a new MetricType (e.g. `BOOLEAN`) is an add-a-class change at both backends. The third value of `metrics.storage` (typo or future expansion) silently produces a zero-extractor configuration that throws `IllegalArgumentException` at request time, not at boot.
  - **Wisdom test**: PASS. The `matchIfMissing=true` placement is the load-bearing positive design choice (default-on for INTERNAL_POSTGRES is deliberate, not an oversight); the mirrored-pair construction is structural (every future MetricType must extend BOTH backends or justify backend-asymmetric support); the dispatcher's linear `filter(canExtract).findFirst()` is itself an extensibility-seam decision (the cost is O(M) per metric-point per call but M is bounded by MetricType count, currently 4).
