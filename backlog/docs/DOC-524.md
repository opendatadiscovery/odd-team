## Description

**Failure shape: reader-flow defect — a cross-reference that looks correct, returns HTTP 200, and silently
does not go where it says.** An anchor miss does not 404. GitBook loads the page and leaves the reader at the
top. On `odd-platform.md` — a ~950-line configuration reference — that is the difference between "here is the
key you need" and "here are all of them, good luck."

Every row below was verified by fetching the destination page from `docs.opendatadiscovery.org` and grepping
the rendered `id=` attribute. The local heading text was **not** trusted as the oracle: GitBook's slugging is
what decides, and it is what disagrees with these links.

### Mechanism A — GitBook keeps the `.` in a dotted config key; the links use `-`

| Link site | Written anchor | Live `id=` |
|---|---|---|
| `odd-platform.md:151`, `:872` | `#session-lifetime-spring-session-timeout` | `session-lifetime-spring.session.timeout` |
| `odd-platform.md:360` | `#prometheus-tenant-label-odd-tenant-id` | `prometheus-tenant-label-odd.tenant-id` |
| `odd-platform.md:846`, `activity-feed.md:153`, `:164` | `#activity-feed-partitioning-odd-activity-partition-period` | `activity-feed-partitioning-odd.activity.partition-period` |
| `odd-platform.md:449`, `:920`, `integration-wizard.md:35`, `:72` | `#odd-platform-base-url` | `odd.platform-base-url` |

Ten link sites, all pointing into the operator's single most-consulted reference. `odd.platform-base-url` and
the activity-feed partition key are the two an operator is most often sent to mid-incident.

### Mechanism B — GitBook collapses `--` to `-`; the links keep the double hyphen

A heading containing ` / ` or ` — ` produces one hyphen, not two.

| Link site | Written anchor | Live `id=` |
|---|---|---|
| `data-lineage.md:13` | `data-objects.md#view-mode-toggle-compact--full` | `view-mode-toggle-compact-full` |
| `developer-guides/api-reference/lineage.md:24` | `data-objects.md#lineage-depth--ui-control-vs-api-contract` | `lineage-depth-ui-control-vs-api-contract` |
| `developer-guides/api-reference/glossary.md:50` | `query-examples.md#per-entity--per-term-lookup-and-linking-6-endpoints` | `per-entity-per-term-lookup-and-linking-6-endpoints` |

### Mechanism C — the destination heading was renamed or removed, and the inbound link was not swept

| Link site | Written anchor | Live reality |
|---|---|---|
| `Architecture.md:43` | `odd-platform.md#attachment-storage` | the section is `attachment-storage-configuration` — and `data-discovery/attachments.md` already links to the correct form, so the right anchor is known and this page is the outlier |
| `alerting.md:28` | `notifications.md#prometheus-alertmanager-inbound-webhook` | the section is `inbound-channel-prometheus-alertmanager-webhook` |
| `permissions.md:97`, `:100` | `owners.md#3-three-service-tier-side-doors-mint-owner-rows-without-owner_create`, `#4-put-apiownersid-with-empty-or-absent-roles-silently-destroys-all-role-bindings` | **no matching or near-matching `id=` on the live `owners` page at all** — establish whether the headings were renamed, renumbered, or removed, and re-point or remove the sentence accordingly |

**Not findings (checked and excluded):** `main-concepts.md#terms-and-aliases` (GitBook renders `&` as `and`
— the links are right); `oauth2-oidc.md#azure-a-d`; and the two backslash-escaped link targets in
`SUMMARY.md:86` and `Architecture.md:9`, which are GitBook's own `\_` escaping and resolve HTTP 200 live.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All ~16 link sites corrected to the anchor the destination page **actually renders**, taken from the
      live `id=` attribute, not from a slug computed by hand.
- [ ] Mechanism C's `owners.md` pair is *resolved*, not merely re-pointed: state whether the headings were
      renamed or removed, and if removed, rewrite the two `permissions.md` sentences so they do not promise a
      section that no longer exists.
- [ ] A repeatable anchor sweep is added to the doc-product audit (`playbooks/doc-product-editorial-read.md`
      mechanical pre-pass, beside the Gate 11 grep) that resolves every `#fragment` against the **rendered**
      page rather than a local guess — the three mechanisms above are all invisible to a source-only check.
- [ ] Post-merge, re-run the sweep: zero broken anchors across `documentation/docs/**`
      (`playbooks/live-site-verification.md`).
