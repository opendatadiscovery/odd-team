## Description

Promote `ADR-CANDIDATE-041`: each channel (slack/webhook/email) activates by the presence of
its `notifications.receivers.X` key via `@ConditionalOnProperty` on the sender bean — the key is
the toggle; absence = no bean = silent no-op; composes with ADR-0040 into a two-stage opt-in;
an empty key is caught by the ADR-0018 fail-fast check.
