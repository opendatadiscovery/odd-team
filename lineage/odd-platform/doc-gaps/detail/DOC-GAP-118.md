    1. **Doc-side primary**: extend `configuration-and-deployment/odd-platform.md`'s housekeeping section with a "Lifecycle interaction with ingestion" admonition: "**Soft-deleted entities are silently restored on the next ingestion tick** if the collector re-emits them.
