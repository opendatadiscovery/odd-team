## What

`AuthIdentityProviderImpl` builds the per-request identity as a `(username, provider)` tuple, but the `provider` field is the OAuth2 client-registration id **only** for OAUTH2 - for LOGIN_FORM, LDAP, and S2S the else-branch hard-codes `provider=null`. Because every owner lookup keys on `OIDC_USERNAME=? AND PROVIDER IS NULL` for all non-OAUTH2 modes, two **genuinely different people** who share a username string across two non-OAUTH2 modes (a LOGIN_FORM `alice` and an LDAP `alice`) resolve to the **same** `OwnerPojo` - and therefore the same My Objects, My Alerts, owner-scoped mutations, and role bindings. The DB partial unique index forces the second person to inherit the first's mapping; they cannot even get a distinct one. The migration step that triggers the takeover (e.g. LOGIN_FORM -> LDAP) is silent: no warning, no fail-fast, no log.

This is the **resolver root-cause** and is **distinct from PLT-065**, which fixes the *read-side* activity-feed JOIN sites (adding `AND PROVIDER=?` to 4 LEFT JOINs). The one-line resolver change at `AuthIdentityProviderImpl.java:32` closes the bleed at the source for all 15 `fetchAssociatedOwner` consumers; PLT-065's JOIN fix is complementary but does not address the resolver. The cross-mode DOC caveat is DOC-GAP-103 / DOC-272.

## Impact

(1) Silent cross-mode identity takeover: after a LOGIN_FORM -> LDAP migration the LDAP person named `alice` lands on a fully-populated account that is actually the previous LOGIN_FORM person's - their data entities, alerts, role-granted permissions, and discussions - with no error and no operator signal. (2) Even without migration, a deployment running LOGIN_FORM and LDAP concurrently with any overlapping username has the same collapse. (3) The whoami endpoint actively hides the problem. This is a confidentiality / least-privilege boundary failure, not a cosmetic bug.

## Suggested fix

Per the reflection's `proposed_fix_anchor`: at `AuthIdentityProviderImpl.java:31-32`, emit distinct provider literals (`"LOGIN_FORM"` / `"LDAP"` / `"S2S"` / `"DISABLED"`) instead of `null` in the else-branch. Existing `(username, null)` rows stay and the DB partial unique index naturally segregates new mappings. Pair with PLT-065's read-side JOIN fix. Confirmatory probe P-227 is emitted to pin the end-to-end bleed.
