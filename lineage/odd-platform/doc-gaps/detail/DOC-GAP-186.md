    1. **Doc-side primary — `documentation/docs/features/management.md`**: correct the live doc's "Tab visibility is permission-aware" claim. Inside Management, only the Associations sub-tab is hidden from users without `OWNER_ASSOCIATION_MANAGE`; all other sub-tabs (Namespaces, Datasources, Integrations, Collectors, Owners, Tags, Policies, Roles) are visible to any signed-in user. This is a deliberate UX convention to avoid permission-aware-styling forks. The trade-off: users without a permission cannot tell from the UI that the affordance exists. They may infer the platform doesn't expose the feature; in fact it does, but for users with higher permission tiers. To check what permissions a feature requires, consult this page's permission-key reference."
