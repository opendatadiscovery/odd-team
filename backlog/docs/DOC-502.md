## Description

ST-6 gives the search box three operators (`"a quoted phrase"`, `-exclusion`, `or`) and, crucially, keeps
**prefix matching on the bare words around them**. Until this ships, `docs/data-discovery/search.md` says the
opposite in two places:

1. It documents no query syntax at all — a reader has no way to learn the operators exist.
2. Its metacharacter caveat states that punctuation is stripped and "the remaining words" are matched as
   prefixes, without mentioning that `"` and a leading `-` become meaningful in 1.0.0.

The new section leads with the rule a user can hold — **bare words match as prefixes; a quoted phrase and an
excluded word match exactly** — because that is the property that distinguishes ODD's implementation from the
obvious one (`websearch_to_tsquery`, which would have revoked prefix matching the moment any operator appeared).
