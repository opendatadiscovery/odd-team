## Description

`docs/Features.md` is the canonical "one-page index" of ODD Platform's features (251 lines, ~35 H2 entries). The intro paragraph (line 3) explicitly positions it as the **quick-scan surface for discovering what the platform does**. After the recent sweep added 4 NEW pages with corresponding SUMMARY.md entries, Features.md was **not updated** to include them.

**The 4 NEW pages and their SUMMARY.md placement:**

| New page | SUMMARY.md entry | Features.md entry |
|---|---|---|
| `multilingual-ui.md` | `* [Multilingual UI](multilingual-ui.md)` — top-level Features section | **MISSING** |
| `data-discovery/entity-description.md` | `* [Entity description](data-discovery/entity-description.md)` — Data Discovery sub-feature | **MISSING** |
| `data-discovery/custom-metadata.md` | `* [Custom metadata](data-discovery/custom-metadata.md)` — Data Discovery sub-feature | **MISSING** |
| `data-discovery/per-column-annotation.md` | `* [Per-column annotation](data-discovery/per-column-annotation.md)` — Data Discovery sub-feature | **MISSING** |

The fifth NEW page from the sweep (`management/namespaces.md`) has 1 incidental mention in Features.md but no dedicated H2 entry; on balance, Namespaces fits the Management pillar's framing rather than warranting a standalone Features.md row, so this item only tracks the 4 above. Add Namespaces if the author prefers symmetry with Lookup Tables (which DOES have its own Features.md row).

**Why this matters:**

1. **Features.md is the operator's at-a-glance discovery surface.** An operator who lands on `docs.opendatadiscovery.org/features` (the highest-ranking root-tier page) and scans the 35-feature list cannot find these 4 features today, even though they ARE features of the platform.
2. **SUMMARY ≠ Features.md.** SUMMARY drives the left-sidebar navigation; Features.md is content. Adding to SUMMARY without adding to Features.md leaves the body in drift.
3. **Pattern parity.** "Manual Object Tagging", "Adding Business Names", "Data Entity Attachments", "Custom navigation links" all have their own Features.md H2 — they are exactly the kind of authoring-surface feature that entity-description, custom-metadata, and per-column-annotation also are. Multilingual UI is a top-level UI capability (gear-icon picker) — it warrants the same prominence as "GenAI assistant" or "Activity Feed for Monitoring Changes".
