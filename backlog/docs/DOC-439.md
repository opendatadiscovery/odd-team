## Description

`dc-data-compliance.md` has an internal contradiction between its Solution paragraph and the closing Scenario step.

- **Solution (line 12)** correctly and explicitly states ODD does **not** classify sensitivity: *"ODD does not classify sensitivity for you: search has no PII or sensitivity facet, and a tag named `PII` is ordinary free text, not a system-recognised sensitivity class. You search to locate the objects, then read each object's tags, labels and metadata — applied by data owners — to judge what is confidential."*
- **Scenario step 6 (line 30)** then reads: *"I find out that `Dim_Customers` and `Dim_Cards` objects cannot be stored. Customer full name, age, address and payment details should be anonymized as these are PII data protected by GDPR and PCI DSS."*

The phrase "I find out that ... are PII" reads as a **system-derived** determination — as if the platform surfaced the PII classification — which is exactly the capability the Solution paragraph just disclaimed. How it misleads an operator: a reader skimming the numbered scenario could conclude ODD detects/flags PII (a sensitivity facet or auto-classification), then waste time looking for that nonexistent feature, or worse, trust a classification the platform never made. The judgement in step 6 is the data scientist's own, made by reading owner-applied tags/labels — the wording should say so, matching the Solution.

(Note: the harvest line reference "step 6 / line 12" maps to the Solution at line 12 and the numbered Scenario item **6** at line 30 — both are cited below.)
