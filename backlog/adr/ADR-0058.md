## Description

Promote `ADR-CANDIDATE-058`: **soft-delete as the deletion model** — `DELETED` is a member of the
closed `DataEntityStatusDto` enum (not a row removal); list/search/lineage hide soft-deletes
(`addSoftDeleteFilter`), the detail read includes them (`includeDeleted`, = `ADR-CANDIDATE-055`
folded), and `DataEntityHousekeepingJob` physically purges DELETED entities + their dependent graph
after a TTL keyed on the status-update timestamp. A 10-minute switch job auto-advances `isSwitchable`
statuses to `DELETED`.
