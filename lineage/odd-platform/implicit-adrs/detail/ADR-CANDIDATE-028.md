  - **Decision statement**: ODD's range-partitioning subsystem for high-volume tables (`activity`, `messages`) is a four-decision composition: (1) **2× forward-coverage WIDTH** — each created partition spans `2 × partitionDaysPeriod` days (default `30`-day period → `60`-day partition width), while a new partition is appended every `partitionDaysPeriod` days; the deliberate 2:1 width-to-cadence ratio eliminates the "no partition for row" window at midnight on cycle-boundary days; (2) **dual-lock concurrency control** — boot-time partition init runs under Postgres advisory lock id `90` (`partition.advisory-lock-id`); nightly cron at `00:01` server-local-time runs under ShedLock with `lockAtLeastFor=10m / lockAtMostFor=10m`; the dual mechanism reflects boot vs. cluster-elected execution contexts; (3) **List-injection extensibility seam** — `PostgreSQLPartitionCreationJob.partitionManagers : List<PartitionManager>` discovers all `@Component`-extending `AbstractPartitionManager` beans automatically; adding a new partitioned table is an add-a-class change; (4) **continue-on-failure orchestration** — per-manager exceptions are caught at the orchestrator (`PostgreSQLPartitionCreationJob.createPartitionIfNotExists` lines 53-60), logged at ERROR, and the outer loop continues to the next manager; the design prioritises maximising partition-creation success across all tables over fail-fast detection of a single-table failure. The composition is deliberate and matches a documented operational contract (live `/configuration-and-deployment/odd-platform` page describes `odd.activity.partition-period`); however, the architectural choices have un-defended consequences (REFACTOR-085: no retention/DROP path is a documented misrepresentation; REFACTOR-086: silent-fail leaves operators blind to partition-creation failure).
  - **Wisdom test**: PASS for all four sub-decisions. (1) The `* 2L` literal multiplier paired with the `+ partitionDaysPeriod` cursor advance is the intent anchor; structural (every partition manager's CREATE arithmetic). (2) The dual-lock construction (advisory-lock + ShedLock) with explicit `LockAssert.assertLocked()` defensive runtime-check is the intent anchor; structural (every future partition table must respect the lock-id namespace). (3) The `List<PartitionManager>` constructor-injection pattern + sibling `MessageTablePartitionManager` consistency is the intent anchor; structural (extensibility seam for new partitioned tables). (4) The try-catch-log-continue at the orchestrator is the intent anchor; structural (the design CHOOSES coverage maximisation over fail-fast); however, the absence of metrics/health-check degradation on top of the continue-on-failure is a gap, not a posture (REFACTOR-086).
