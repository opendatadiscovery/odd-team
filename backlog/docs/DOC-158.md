## Description

Editorial-audit finding from `/review feature/docs-features-md-carve-out-2026-05-07` (2026-05-07).

Two sentences on `main-concepts.md` cross-link to `Features.md` as a whole (no anchor) instead of pointing at the canonical bucket landing or detail page for the content being referenced. With the carve-out shipping `data-discovery/schema-diff.md` as a new dedicated detail page (commit `d150fa3`, PR #68), the L68 reference's canonical destination is now sharper than before — this is the right moment to retarget both sites.

These are **not** reverse-link-to-Features.md-anchor violations (the rule DOC-149/150/154 cleaned up). They cross-link to Features.md *as a whole*, which the no-reverse-link rule technically permits (it bans reverse-links to `Features.md#anchor` for canonical reference). But Cornerstone 5 (one canonical home per content type) makes the broader case: a vocabulary-surface page (`main-concepts.md`) pointing at the Features index is a weaker cross-link than pointing at the canonical bucket landing or detail page. The Features index is not a content home; it is the navigation surface that points at the homes.

### Site 1 — `main-concepts.md` L9 (Business Glossary disambiguation callout)

> *"**Not the Business Glossary.** ODD Platform ships an in-app **Business Glossary** feature (term entities you can link to datasets, term-to-term relationships, ownership). That is a different thing from this docs page. See the Business Glossary section of [Features.md](Features.md) for the product feature."*

The disambiguation callout warns readers that `main-concepts.md` is not the in-app Business Glossary feature. The reader who needs the in-app feature should land on `data-glossary/business-glossary.md` (the canonical home for the Business Glossary feature per Cornerstone 2 + Cornerstone 5). Pointing at Features.md sends them to the index; one extra click before they reach what they need.

Resolution: retarget the cross-link to `data-glossary/business-glossary.md`.

### Site 2 — `main-concepts.md` L68 (Data Modeling availability callout)

> *"- **Data Modeling** — *partially available.* [Data Entity Groups](data-discovery/groups-domains.md) (DEGs) for logical grouping and entity relationship / ERD views today. Schema evolution signals (backwards-incompatible change triggers) are surfaced in alerts. See the DEG / schema-diff sections in [Features.md](Features.md) and the [Data Modelling](data-modelling.md) pillar."*

The "DEG / schema-diff sections in Features.md" cross-link references content whose canonical homes are now:

- DEG content → `data-discovery/groups-domains.md` (already cross-linked at L68's start, so the back-half is duplicate).
- schema-diff content → `data-discovery/schema-diff.md` (the new dedicated detail page from PR #68 — did not exist when L68 was authored).

Resolution: replace "the DEG / schema-diff sections in [Features.md]" with "[Dataset schema diff](data-discovery/schema-diff.md)" (DEG already cross-linked earlier in the same sentence; no need to repeat). The result reads cleanly: *"…[Data Entity Groups](data-discovery/groups-domains.md) (DEGs) for logical grouping and entity relationship / ERD views today. Schema evolution signals (backwards-incompatible change triggers) are surfaced in alerts. See [Dataset schema diff](data-discovery/schema-diff.md) and the [Data Modelling](data-modelling.md) pillar."*

## Context

- **Why low priority.** Both sites are narrative cross-references in callout-style sentences, not authoritative reference. A reader hitting the Features.md link arrives at correct content (the index does point at the canonical home in turn — one extra click). The defect is *cross-link directness* (Cornerstone 5 — point at the canonical home, not at the index that points at it).
- **Why this is the right finding to log.** Same shape as DOC-156 (use-case pages bypassing canonical homes for GitHub URLs), but on a different surface: `main-concepts.md` (the vocabulary surface) bypassing canonical homes by pointing at the index instead. Cornerstone 5 is the rule both items enforce.
- **Why the carve-out surfaced this.** Pre-carve-out, the L68 reference to "schema-diff sections in Features.md" was the only home for schema-diff content (Features.md L147-185 was the canonical home). Post-carve-out, schema-diff has a dedicated detail page, so pointing at Features.md is now strictly weaker than pointing at the dedicated page. The editorial audit caught the now-improvable site.
- **Pre-existing defect, not introduced by this batch.** The L9 disambiguation predates the carve-out (it always referenced Features.md as a whole). The L68 reference was authored when schema-diff lived inline on Features.md. Both are pre-existing patterns now made fixable by the carve-out's structural changes.
