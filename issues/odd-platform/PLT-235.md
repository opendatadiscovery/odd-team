## Description

Carved out of the Term Detail hardening epic (#1754 / PLT-058, Defect 3). The Term Detail page exposes the
term↔term relation through **two surfaces with different affordances**:

- **Overview → TERMS mini-panel** (`Overview/TermLinkedTerms/TermLinkedTerms.tsx`, gated `TERM_UPDATE`): Add
  (via `LinkedTermTermForm`) + per-item Delete (`removeTerm`). No search.
- **Linked-Terms tab** (`TermLinkedTermsList/LinkedTermsList.tsx`): search + a paginated list. **No Add/Delete.**

An operator learns Add/Delete on Overview, then cannot perform them on the tab where they go for bulk/browse
work. Same relation, two inconsistent control sets — operator-visible confusion (verified live during CTRIB-028).

This is **deferred from CTRIB-028 because it requires a product/UX decision**, not a mechanical fix.

## Options (a product decision — pick one)

- **(a) Consolidate on the tab:** Overview panel becomes a read-only summary + "see all" link; the Linked-Terms
  tab carries all affordances (Add/Delete/Search). One canonical management surface.
- **(b) Make the dual-surface intentional + legible:** rename the Overview panel "Recent linked terms" (quick-add
  shortcut); document that the tab is the canonical browse/manage surface. Keep both, remove the ambiguity.

Recommend (a) — a single canonical surface matches the other reverse-lookup tabs and ODD's one-home principle —
but this is the maintainer's product call.

## Acceptance

- The two surfaces present a consistent, non-confusing affordance model (per the chosen option).
- If (a): the Linked-Terms tab supports Add/Delete (gated `TERM_UPDATE`) and the Overview panel links to it.
