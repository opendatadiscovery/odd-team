## Description

The Catalog search result row is mounted as a whole-row click handler, not as a link. `ResultItem.tsx:71-76` renders `<S.Container ... onClick={() => navigate(detailsLink)}>` — a `div`-based MUI `Grid` Container with a JS `navigate`, no `<a href={detailsLink}>` and no `<button>`. Two operator-facing consequences, both already homed as bug Defects 3 & 4 of PLT-091 (the search-UX cluster):

- **H-004 (verdict `contradicted`, HIGH):** a user who wants to open a result in a background tab (middle-click / Ctrl+click) cannot — the universal browse-many-then-read gesture relies on a real anchor, and a JS `onClick` does not fire on middle-click and gives no `href` for the browser to open. There is no native new-tab affordance. (Fold into PLT-091 Defect 3 — "no real `<a>`/keyboard/role".)
- **H-005 (verdict `probe-needed`):** inner clickable children mounted in the row — `<MetadataStale>` (`ResultItem.tsx:87-90`), the search-highlights `<AppTooltip><QuestionIcon/></AppTooltip>` (`:101-105`), and `<DataEntityDetailsPreview>` (`:106`) — do not visibly call `event.stopPropagation()` at the consumer site, so a click on an inner affordance may bubble to the row-level `onClick` and double-navigate (compounding the view_count `+2` write of PLT-091 Defect 2). Whether each child actually swallows the click is not statically decidable here → a probe is required. (Fold into PLT-091 Defect 4.)

**Typed gate: characterization-pin** (LSN-029). The new-tab assertion pins the CURRENT (operator-undesirable) structure — the row exposes no `href`/anchor — and goes RED the day the row becomes a real `<a>` (the planned PLT-091 fix). It is GREEN today and is NOT `@Disabled`. The inner-child portion is probe-gated, not a static assertion: it stays a probe demand until the bubble behaviour is observed.

Note on dedup: the repeat-look / double-count view_count inflation is the KNOWN, already-tracked PLT-104 (and PLT-091 Defect 2) — this item does NOT re-file it; it pins only the anchor/new-tab structure (H-004) and demands the inner-child bubble probe (H-005).
