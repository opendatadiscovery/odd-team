## Description

`documentation/docs/data-discovery/search.md:93` documents how the search box treats `tsquery` metacharacters —
introduced by DOC-460 for 0.28.0 and live-verified `done`. It is accurate as far as it goes, and **incomplete in
two ways that matter to an operator**, both about the currently published release:

**1. The character list omits `"` and `-`.** The caveat enumerates ``( ) : & | ! '`` as the characters that are
"not searchable literals". `"` and `-` are also not searchable literals — they are silently dropped or ignored —
but a reader checking the list will conclude they are safe to type.

**2. `-term` does the OPPOSITE of what a user expects, and the page does not say so.** Measured first-hand on
`postgres:13.2-alpine` (the deployed version) against an exact emulation of `JooqFTSHelper.tsQuery`:

| Typed | Actual tsquery today | What the user gets |
|---|---|---|
| `customer -test` | `'custom':* & 'test':*` | assets that **contain** `test` — the dash is dropped and the word becomes **required** |
| `"customer orders"` | word-AND, quotes discarded | both words anywhere, not the phrase |
| `customer or orders` | `'custom':* & 'order':*` | assets containing **both**, not either |

Every mainstream search box treats `-x` as an exclusion, so an operator typing `customer -test` to *remove* test
assets receives exactly the set they were trying to remove, with no signal that the operator was ignored. That is
a documentation gap with a wrong-results consequence, not a cosmetic omission.
