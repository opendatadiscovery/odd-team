**The 4-layer coverage anchors the finding's UNIVERSALITY**: the absence of authorization is not a method-level oversight; it is a CLASS-WIDE, SERVICE-WIDE, AND HANDLER-INTERFACE-WIDE absence. Adding `@PreAuthorize` to a single method (e.g. `getActivity` only) would be insufficient — the service-tier methods called by the controller (`getActivityList`, `getActivityCounts`, `getDataEntityActivityList`) all have ZERO authorization checks at the service tier, so a future controller-method that calls them ALSO bypasses authorization. The structural fix requires either (a) a service-tier `@PreAuthorize` (matching the pattern of RoleServiceImpl + OwnerServiceImpl + DataSourceIngestionServiceImpl), OR (b) a controller-tier `@PreAuthorize` plus a service-tier guard that asserts the SecurityContext holds the expected permission, OR (c) a `SecurityRule` entry that gates the path at the WebFilterChain layer.
