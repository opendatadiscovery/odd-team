## Description

Promote `ADR-CANDIDATE-042`: `AlertNotificationMessageProcessor` catches a per-channel
`NotificationSenderException`, logs ERROR, and continues to the next sender — one bad channel
never blocks the others or stalls the WAL. 📌 Consequences note the trade-off (partial failure
is log-only); the gap-shaped consequence (no delivery audit/metric) stays in
`refactoring-scopes.md`, not printed on the page.
