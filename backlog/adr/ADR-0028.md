## Description

Promote `ADR-CANDIDATE-028`: a nightly ShedLock-elected job creates double-width range
partitions ahead of cadence for the activity (and Data-Collaboration message) tables, discovers
partition managers via `List<PartitionManager>` injection, and continues past any one table's
failure. Create-only — no DROP/retention path here (that is housekeeping, ADR-0045).
