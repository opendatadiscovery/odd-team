The query is interpreted as a `to_tsquery` argument verbatim — meaning: (a) special characters with tsquery meaning (`!`, `|`, `(`, `)`, `<->`, `:`) NOT preceded by a space will produce a syntax error response; (b) queries containing balanced tsquery expressions will be interpreted by Postgres as those expressions; (c) no escape mechanism is currently exposed — operators needing to search for literal special characters should rely on the facet filters (Tag, Owner, Namespace, etc.) instead."
