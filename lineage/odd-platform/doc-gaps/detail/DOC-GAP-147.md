- **DOC-GAP-147**: NotificationsDispatcher per-sender exception-type asymmetry — Slack and Webhook senders throw the checked `NotificationSenderException` (caught by the per-sender try/catch in the dispatcher; loop continues to next sender). `EmailNotificationSender` wraps its `MessagingException | TemplateException | IOException` chain as a `new RuntimeException(...)` (`EmailNotificationSender.java:59`) — NOT a `NotificationSenderException`. Email failures therefore BYPASS the dispatcher's per-sender catch and ABORT fan-out for that message; if Email is not the last sender in `List<NotificationSender>`, subsequent senders do NOT receive the alert. This contradicts the implicit "catch-and-continue" ADR for one channel only and the live Notifications page's framing of partial-delivery as a per-recipient (not per-channel-type) behaviour.

  - **Proposed doc action**: Add to `features/active-platform-features/notifications.md` (under the existing partial-delivery discussion) a "Cross-channel failure behaviour and exception-type asymmetry" admonition: "**The cross-channel partial-delivery contract is asymmetric**: Slack and Webhook channel failures are isolated (the next sender in the fan-out receives the alert as expected). Email channel failures of certain classes (`MessagingException`, `TemplateException`, `IOException`) are wrapped as raw `RuntimeException` and propagate OUT of the dispatcher's per-channel error handler — aborting fan-out for the message. If your `List<NotificationSender>` order is `[Slack, Webhook, Email]` and Email fails, the message has already been delivered to Slack and Webhook; if the order is `[Slack, Email, Webhook]` and Email fails, Webhook does NOT receive the message. The order depends on Spring's bean-registration order and is not operator-configurable today. To minimise the impact, expect that an Email-channel hard failure (SMTP unreachable, template rendering error, attachment IO error) may suppress delivery on one or more channels registered after Email. Recommended mitigation: until upstream fix, prefer Slack/Webhook over Email if your delivery SLA cannot tolerate this asymmetry; OR file `/log-issue odd-platform` for an `EmailNotificationSender` change wrapping its checked exceptions as `NotificationSenderException`."
