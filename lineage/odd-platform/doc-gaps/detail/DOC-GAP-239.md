       > **Payload shape — `DatasetStatisticsList`**: a list of `DataSetStatistics`, each with `dataset_oddrn` (string, the parent dataset's ODDRN) and `fields: Map<fieldOddrn, DataSetFieldStat>` (a map from each dataset field's ODDRN to its statistical profile). A payload's field ODDRNs may target fields in datasets other than the declared parent. Custom DQ-push integrations MUST validate parent-child consistency on the client side.
