## Description

Promote `ADR-CANDIDATE-028`: a nightly ShedLock-elected job creates double-width range
partitions ahead of cadence for the activity (and Data-Collaboration message) tables, discovers
partition managers via `List<PartitionManager>` injection, and continues past any one table's
failure. Create-only — no DROP/retention path here (that is housekeeping, ADR-0045).

- **SELF-CORRECTION (the load-bearing find — my error, not the candidate's).** My first draft of
  this page claimed the partition subsystem has "no advisory lock and no boot hook — a single
  nightly ShedLock cron", and framed that as *refuting* the candidate. **That was wrong.** A full
  `Read` of `PostgreSQLPartitionCreationJob.java` at `ede5d277` shows it has BOTH: a
  `@PostConstruct init()` (`:29-38`) that acquires a Postgres advisory lock
  (`@Value("${partition.advisory-lock-id}")`, default `90`, `:26-27`) at boot, AND a nightly
  `@Scheduled(cron="0 1 0 * * *")` `run()` under `@SchedulerLock(name="partitionCreationJob")`
  (`:40-43`). **The candidate's "dual-lock" framing was correct;** my first draft was authored
  from a stale prior-session grep before this session's full read landed, and I committed the
  wrong version with a confident false "refutation". The page is now rewritten to the verified
  dual-trigger mechanism and re-pushed.
