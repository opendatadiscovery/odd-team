  - **Decision statement**: ODD's LDAP admin-group enforcement decides membership via case-insensitive **substring containment** rather than exact equality. The match logic at `LDAPSecurityConfiguration.java:94-98` reads `adminGroups.stream().anyMatch(adminGroup -> containsIgnoreCase(authority.getAuthority(), adminGroup))`. The decision codifies an operator-ergonomic short-form: operators configure `admin-groups: ['Admins']` and the match succeeds against the LDAP-returned full DN (`cn=Admins,ou=Groups,dc=example,dc=com`) without requiring the operator to write out the full DN. The trade-off accepted: (a) easier operator onboarding — short-form admin-group names work out of the box; (b) **substring-collision admin escalation** — REFACTOR-119 captures the case where `admin-groups: ['ops']` silently matches `cn=devops` or `cn=oopsgroup` and admits unintended users to ADMIN. The maintainer's choice to use `containsIgnoreCase` over `Set::contains` IS intentional (the alternative method is `Set::contains` / `List::contains` with case-insensitive comparator — that would have required either pre-normalisation or a custom comparator). The deliberate selection of substring containment is the architectural decision; the substring-collision consequence is the gap.

Per Rule 0 split, the ergonomic intent stays here as the ADR; REFACTOR-119 captures the gap.
