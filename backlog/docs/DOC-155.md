## Description

Editorial-audit finding from `/review batch:feature/docs-doc149-cleanup-batch-2026-05-07` 2026-05-07.

DOC-153 consolidated the six-pillar differentiation framing to `main-concepts.md#pillar-differentiation` (canonical home) and updated five of the six governance pillar landings to carry a `## Why this is a separate pillar` section with a one-sentence cross-link to that home plus a short pillar-specific hook:

| `data-modelling.md` | **✗ (missing)** | **✗ (missing)** |

`data-modelling.md` is the older pillar landing (created in DOC-048 — the first pillar landing — and never gained a differentiation framing). DOC-153's AC line 65 explicitly named both `data-modelling.md` and `master-data-management.md` as in scope: *"Verify `data-modelling.md` and `master-data-management.md` carry analogous sections and update them too — the four-pillar list above doesn't include them but they likely have similar content from earlier batches."* The DOC-153 implementer updated `master-data-management.md` (which had a preexisting "Why this is a separate pillar from Data Modelling" section to consolidate) but did not add a corresponding section to `data-modelling.md` (which had no preexisting section to consolidate). Per CLAUDE.md velocity principle that was a defensible call mid-batch — but per the world-class-doc bar (Cornerstone 4 + `pillars/documentation/pillar.md` "no middle ground"), 6-of-6 pillar-landing consistency is the higher standard. A reader landing on `data-modelling.md` from `main-concepts.md#data-governance-map` finds no cross-link back to the canonical pillar-differentiation surface; every peer pillar landing offers that surface explicitly.

The fix is small (~10 lines) and matches the shape used by the other 5 landings: a `## Why this is a separate pillar` H2 with a one-sentence cross-link plus a short hook capturing what makes Data Modelling distinct (per `main-concepts.md#pillar-differentiation` L79: *"Data Modelling is contract-oriented — describing how a dataset is queried (Query Examples) and connected (Relationships / ERDs). The dataset itself comes from outside; the platform records intent and structure on top."*).

- **Why medium (not low).** The defect is mild — operators reading `data-modelling.md` are not misled, just under-served compared to peers. But the pillar-landing consistency *is* the doc-product's structural promise post-IA-refactor; a 5-of-6 implementation is exactly the "scattered intent" failure mode `pillars/documentation/cornerstones.md` Cornerstone 2 + `pillars/documentation/pillar.md` "What this pillar maintains" exists to catch. The reviewer's editorial audit caught it on the next pass. The systemic question is whether AC items that say "verify and update" should require explicit `verified — no change needed` or `verified — fix in DOC-NNN` close-out notes; if a similar shape recurs, log an LSN.

Every other pillar landing offers that back-link explicitly. The IA expectation per Cornerstone 2 is that bucket landings cross-link outward to the index-level vocabulary surface (main-concepts.md), not the reverse — this fix completes that pattern on the 6th and final pillar landing.
